Gaslight to Deco

This prototype tests one era change: Industrial into Machine Age, at 1900. Scroll down. Colours, corners, borders and texture blend continuously with your scroll position. The big stories get full cards; everything else is a one-line row on the spine that opens when you tap it.

Nothing readable sits in the middle of the blend. The seam is tall enough that no cards are on screen while the page is mid-grey, the caption fades out through the middle, and text colour and the year counter switch over at the midpoint.
